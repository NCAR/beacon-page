Wildfire, utilities and insurance: 14 March 2025 Southern Plains outbreak

Compare ECMWF AIFS (AI) with HRRR (full physics) for high winds, low humidity, and the duration of dangerous fire-weather conditions, in the 14 March 2025 Southern Plains outbreak. SPC rated eastern New Mexico, the Texas Panhandle and Oklahoma Extremely Critical; Oklahoma had its windiest day in Mesonet history, with gusts to 90 mph. BEACON retrieves forecasts and observations and evaluates threshold exceedance and warning lead time relevant to utility preparedness and wildfire exposure.

Not yet

  • Gusts: Utilities decide power shutoffs on gusts; HRRR forecasts them, AIFS doesn't.
  • Fuel dryness: Red Flag decisions also weigh fuels; BEACON compares weather only.

Dangerous fire weather is wind of at least 20 mph with RH of 15 % or less at the same station and hour, for at least 2 hours, with the forecast onset within 6 hours of the observed one. The onset is the first hour of the first spell of 2 dangerous hours in a row, not a lone dangerous hour before it. NWS offices and utilities set their own values, and Red Flag criteria use 20 ft winds where BEACON compares 10 m winds. AIFS has values every 6 hours, so it is judged only where the observations at those times show the event starting within 6 hours of the observed onset. AIFS has no humidity output, so BEACON computes it from AIFS temperature and dewpoint.

Extreme rainfall and urban flooding: 9-10 August 2025 Milwaukee

Compare two AI models (GraphCast-GFS and ECMWF AIFS) with two physics models (GFS and HRRR) for the 6-hour rainfall of the 9-10 August 2025 Milwaukee flood, against two rainfall analyses (Stage IV and URMA). Several rounds of thunderstorms dropped 1 to 3 inches an hour for several hours, mostly between 6 pm on the 9th and 3 am on the 10th. A rain gauge on James Madison High School in northwest Milwaukee measured 14.55 inches in 21 hours, a new 24-hour record for Wisconsin. The Kinnickinnic, Milwaukee, Menomonee and Root rivers reached record levels, and Milwaukee County declared a state of emergency. BEACON scores where and how much each model put the heavy rain, at leads out to 5 days, and maps it against the analyses.

From BEACON Steering Committee pilot "Extreme rainfall / urban flooding" (2026-11-13), which asks for several models and several observations. Event facts from NWS Milwaukee/Sullivan ("New Wisconsin state precipitation record"), the UW-Madison Nelson Institute ("Historic flooding in southeast Wisconsin: August 9 to 10, 2025") and the AP (11 August 2025), checked 2026-11-20.

Not yet

  • Hourly rain rates: The flooding came from 1 to 3 inches an hour, but only HRRR forecasts hourly totals, so every model is compared on 6-hour totals.
  • Event report: Onset and warning lead time at places in the city, as the fire-weather case reports at stations. The station observations BEACON uses (GDAS) have no rainfall; this needs the analyses read at points instead.
  • Neighborhood scores: A 3 km model that puts a storm a few kilometres off misses it point by point. Fractions skill scores (Grid-Stat's neighborhood output) allow for that, but the GUI doesn't show them yet.
  • Independent observations: URMA's rainfall is built from the River Forecast Centers' analyses that also make up Stage IV, so the two share most of their inputs. MRMS radar estimates, rain gauges (CoCoRaHS) and satellite estimates (CMORPH) would add independent observations.

GFS and GraphCast-GFS start from the same GDAS analysis, so they compare a physics model with an AI model from one starting point. GFS and GraphCast-GFS write 6-hour totals; HRRR and AIFS write totals since the run started, so MET PCPCombine subtracts the total 6 hours earlier. Every model and both analyses are put on the box by MET's budget method, which keeps rainfall totals. The maps' heavy-rain area is 6-hour rain of at least 2 inches. Most of the box is outside the city, so the MKE region scores the metro area alone.

METplus configs

GridStat.conf: PCPCombine works out the amounts of forecasts that write only run totals, then Grid-Stat puts every forecast and analysis on the map box and scores them, keeping the maps for the BEACON Report.
