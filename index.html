Wildfire, utilities and insurance: 14 March 2025 Southern Plains outbreak

Compare ECMWF AIFS (AI) with HRRR (full physics) for high winds, low humidity, and the duration of dangerous fire-weather conditions, in the 14 March 2025 Southern Plains outbreak. SPC rated eastern New Mexico, the Texas Panhandle and Oklahoma Extremely Critical; Oklahoma had its windiest day in Mesonet history, with gusts to 90 mph. BEACON retrieves forecasts and observations and evaluates threshold exceedance and warning lead time relevant to utility preparedness and wildfire exposure.

From docs/surveyResponse.md, 1. End-to-end BEACON run; event and settings from UTILITIES.md, step 0 (2026-11-02).

Not yet

  • Gusts: Utilities decide power shutoffs on gusts; HRRR forecasts them, AIFS doesn't.
  • Fuel dryness: Red Flag decisions also weigh fuels; BEACON compares weather only.

Dangerous fire weather is wind of at least 20 mph with RH of 15 % or less at the same station and hour, for at least 2 hours, with the forecast onset within 6 hours of the observed one. The onset is the first hour of the first spell of 2 dangerous hours in a row, not a lone dangerous hour before it. NWS offices and utilities set their own values, and Red Flag criteria use 20 ft winds where BEACON compares 10 m winds. AIFS has values every 6 hours, so it is judged only where the observations at those times show the event starting within 6 hours of the observed onset. AIFS has no humidity output, so BEACON computes it from AIFS temperature and dewpoint.

METplus configs

PB2NC.conf converts the station reports; PointStat.conf matches every forecast to the same stations.

PointStatEvent.conf repeats the matching at the event stations only and keeps every forecast and observed value, for the event report.

Extreme heat

Compare AIFS (AI) with HRRR (full physics) for temperature, humidity, and heat-event onset and duration. BEACON retrieves forecasts and observations and evaluates hazardous threshold exceedance. Heat index or WBGT could be included where the necessary inputs and reference measurements are available.

Not yet

  • Heat index and WBGT: Both have to be computed per model and for observations; WBGT also needs radiation, which both models forecast.
  • Onset and duration of heat events: Needs hour-by-hour series; AIFS writes output every 6 hours.
  • Overnight minimum temperature: Needs the minimum over each night, not 6-hourly values.

Valid times are 00, 06, 12 and 18 UTC. 18Z is late morning in the West and early afternoon in the East; 00Z is late afternoon in the West and evening in the East. The daily peak is often between them until hourly comparisons are added.
